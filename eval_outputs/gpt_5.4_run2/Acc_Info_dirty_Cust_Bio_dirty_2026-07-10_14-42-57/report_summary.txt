This report covers two related customer-level datasets from a subscription-based digital service context: one captures operational account records, while the other holds customer biographical and contact information. They are linked through a shared S_N identifier that appears to support a largely one-to-one join, allowing account activity, subscription, status, device, and feedback details to be combined with customer identity, demographic, and address information; a separate path between account and identity-number fields should be treated only as a candidate linkage route for comparison or record matching, not as a confirmed join. Together, the tables support customer administration, account and subscription analysis, service reporting, and customer profiling in a more complete person-and-account view. At a glance, Acc_Info_dirty contains 30 rows and 15 columns, and Cust_Bio_dirty contains 30 rows and 12 columns, with information grouped broadly into identifiers, lifecycle and activity details, subscription and financial attributes, status and preferences, feedback measures, and customer biography, contact, and location details.
