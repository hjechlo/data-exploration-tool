Exact resemblance measures how much the distinct values in two columns overlap, while shingle resemblance measures fuzzy character-level similarity and is mainly useful for text fields such as names or addresses; directional containment instead asks whether values from one column are contained within the other, which is the key signal for true foreign key relationships. In this result set, Acc_Info_dirty.S_N and Cust_Bio_dirty.S_N are the only confirmed key-style relationship, appearing as a one-to-one shared key with very strong overlap, although the small unmatched portion on Cust_Bio_dirty.S_N should be checked before relying on it operationally. By contrast, Acc_Info_dirty.ID and Cust_Bio_dirty.ID_No is only an exploratory candidate linkage path from MinHash and should be treated as a possible linkage or review field rather than a confirmed primary/foreign key join. A final caveat is that low or moderate exact resemblance by itself does not rule out a valid numeric foreign key when directional containment is high, and it does not mean fuzzy matching is needed for numeric ID columns.
