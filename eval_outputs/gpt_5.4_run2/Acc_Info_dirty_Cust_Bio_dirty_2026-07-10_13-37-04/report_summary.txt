This report covers two related customer-level datasets in a subscription-based digital service context: one focused on account administration and service usage, and the other on customer biographical and contact details. The tables are linked by a shared S_N identifier that appears to support a largely one-to-one relationship, with the account table contributing lifecycle, subscription, balance, activity, device, language, and feedback information and the biography table contributing identity, demographic, contact, and address details; together they support a more complete view of each customer for operations, segmentation, and customer analysis. There is also a candidate linkage path between account and personal identifier fields that may be useful for comparison or record linkage, but it should not be treated as a confirmed join. At a glance, Acc_Info_dirty contains 30 rows and 15 columns, Cust_Bio_dirty contains 30 rows and 12 columns, and the collection is organized around customer identifiers, profile and contact information, account lifecycle and status, subscription and financial attributes, usage environment, and feedback-related measures.
