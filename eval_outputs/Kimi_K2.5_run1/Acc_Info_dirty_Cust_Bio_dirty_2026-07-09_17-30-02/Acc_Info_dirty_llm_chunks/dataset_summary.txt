This dataset comprises individual customer account records for a subscription-based digital service, capturing personal identifiers, subscription details, platform usage metrics, and satisfaction indicators. It supports customer relationship management, subscription analytics, and user behavior analysis for tracking account lifecycles, service utilization patterns, and customer segmentation. The dataset contains 30 rows and 15 columns, with both the ID and Full_Name fields serving as unique table-level identifiers. The structure encompasses demographic and identity attributes, subscription tier and billing information, temporal activity markers, technical platform specifications, and customer feedback metrics.
