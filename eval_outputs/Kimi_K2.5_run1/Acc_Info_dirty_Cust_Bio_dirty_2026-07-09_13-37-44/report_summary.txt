This data dictionary describes an integrated collection of customer records spanning operational account management and biographical identity data for users of a subscription-based digital service based in Singapore. The two tables share a common unique identifier that supports a near-complete one-to-one linkage, enabling analysts to connect subscription characteristics and platform usage patterns with demographic attributes and residential address details, while an additional candidate identifier pair offers potential for fuzzy matching or record linkage to reconcile identity discrepancies. Acc_Info_dirty contains 30 rows and 15 columns covering subscription tiers, temporal activity markers, technical specifications, and account balances, while Cust_Bio_dirty comprises 30 rows and 12 columns organized around personal identification, demographic segmentation, and Singapore-specific contact information.
