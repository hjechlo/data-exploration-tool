This dataset contains biographical and contact information for individual customers based in Singapore, capturing personal identifiers, demographic characteristics, and residential address details. It supports customer relationship management, identity verification, and demographic segmentation for systems requiring population-level analysis or direct customer communication. The table comprises 30 rows and 12 columns, with the S_N column serving as a unique table-level identifier, and organizes data into four main groupings: personal identification fields, demographic attributes, contact information, and Singapore street address details.
