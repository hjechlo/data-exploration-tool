This dataset comprises account records for individual users of a subscription-based digital service, capturing personal identifiers, subscription details, and platform usage characteristics. The data supports customer lifecycle analysis, subscription tier evaluation, and user engagement monitoring for account management or CRM systems. Structured across 30 rows and 15 columns, the dataset contains a unique table-level identifier in the ID field alongside personal attributes, temporal enrollment and activity timestamps, subscription classifications with associated fees, account balances, technical device specifications, and customer feedback ratings.
