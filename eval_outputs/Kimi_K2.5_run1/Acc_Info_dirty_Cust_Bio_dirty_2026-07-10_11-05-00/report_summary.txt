This data dictionary describes a paired collection of customer records for a subscription-based digital service, combining account activity and subscription details with biographical profiles and contact information. The two tables share the S_N identifier as a confirmed one-to-one join key that links service usage patterns to demographic attributes and geographic location data, while candidate linkage paths based on national identification numbers may support fuzzy matching or record linkage workflows for identity verification. Together these datasets provide a foundation for analyzing customer engagement, subscription analytics, and regional distribution across 30 records each, with the first table containing 15 columns organized around account events, subscription classifications, and device specifications, and the second comprising 12 columns covering personal demographics, contact details, and residential address components.
