This dataset comprises individual user account records for a subscription-based digital service, capturing customer identity information including full names and national identification numbers alongside demographic attributes such as preferred language. The data appears designed to support customer relationship management and subscription analytics, enabling analysis of user engagement patterns, subscription tier distributions, and account activity timelines across different device platforms and operating systems. Structured with 30 rows and 15 columns, the dataset contains unique table-level identifiers in both the Full_Name and ID fields, with column groupings encompassing personal identifiers, temporal account events like join dates and last logins, subscription classification and billing details, financial balances, technical device specifications, and user satisfaction metrics.
