This data dictionary describes paired customer datasets from a subscription-based digital service within a Singapore consumer services domain, capturing account activity and detailed demographic profiles. The tables share a confirmed one-to-one join key on S_N with near-complete coverage, while ID and ID_No offer a candidate linkage path for alternative matching; Acc_Info_dirty supplies subscription details, device usage, and satisfaction metrics, whereas Cust_Bio_dirty provides demographic attributes and contact information, together enabling integrated analysis of user behavior against identity and location. Both tables contain 30 rows, with Acc_Info_dirty spanning 15 columns of account and technical data and Cust_Bio_dirty organized into 12 columns of personal and address information.
