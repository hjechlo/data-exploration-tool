This dataset contains individual customer account records for a subscription-based digital service, capturing personal identifiers, demographic attributes, and platform usage details. The data supports customer relationship management, subscription tier analysis, and user behavior tracking across different devices and languages. The table comprises 30 rows and 15 columns, with the ID field serving as a unique table-level identifier. The columns are organized into thematic groupings encompassing personal identification and demographics, subscription and financial details, account activity timestamps, technical device specifications, and customer satisfaction metrics.
