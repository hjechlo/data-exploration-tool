This data dictionary describes a paired collection of customer records for a Singapore-based subscription digital service, spanning account operational data and biographical contact information. The datasets relate through a confirmed one-to-one key on S_N, enabling precise linkage of subscription configurations and activity logs with demographic details and residential addresses, while additional candidate identifier fields support fuzzy matching or record linkage exploration between the tables. Combined, they provide a foundation for customer relationship management, subscriber segmentation, churn prediction, and identity verification workflows. Acc_Info_dirty comprises 30 rows and 15 columns covering account lifecycle timestamps, subscription tiers, financial balances, technical preferences, and satisfaction metrics, while Cust_Bio_dirty comprises 30 rows and 12 columns organized around personal identifiers, demographic attributes, contact details, and location information.
