This dataset captures individual customer account records for a subscription-based digital service, containing personal identifiers, subscription configurations, and activity logs that suggest a Singapore-based operation given the NRIC and FIN format of the identification numbers. The data supports customer relationship management and business intelligence applications such as subscriber segmentation, revenue analysis, churn prediction, and user engagement monitoring across various service tiers. Comprising 30 rows and 15 columns, the dataset employs the ID field as a unique table-level identifier and organizes attributes into thematic groupings: personal identity details, account lifecycle timestamps, subscription tier and billing information, financial account balances, technical preferences covering device and operating system, and customer satisfaction metrics.
