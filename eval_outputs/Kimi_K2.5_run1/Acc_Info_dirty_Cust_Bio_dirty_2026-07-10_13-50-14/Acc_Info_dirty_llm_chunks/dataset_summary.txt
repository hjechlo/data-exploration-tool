This dataset captures individual user account records for a subscription-based digital service, containing personal identifiers, subscription details, and platform usage characteristics. The domain suggests a Singapore-specific context indicated by the national identity number formats present. The data supports customer lifecycle management and subscription revenue analysis, enabling examination of membership tiers, account activity patterns, and platform preferences to inform churn prediction and user segmentation strategies. The dataset contains 30 rows and 15 columns with the ID field serving as a unique table-level identifier, organized thematically into personal identification attributes, subscription and financial details, temporal activity markers, and technical usage indicators.
