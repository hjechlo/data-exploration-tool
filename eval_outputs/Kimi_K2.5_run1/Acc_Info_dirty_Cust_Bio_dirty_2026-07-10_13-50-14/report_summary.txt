This collection comprises two datasets documenting Singapore-based subscription service customers, spanning account activity and biographical profiles. The tables connect through a confirmed one-to-one relationship on the S_N identifier, enabling integration of subscription behavior with demographic and contact details, while candidate linkage paths between additional identifier fields may support fuzzy matching or record linkage validation. Acc_Info_dirty contributes subscription characteristics and platform usage patterns across 15 columns, while Cust_Bio_dirty supplies demographic attributes and address information across 12 columns, with each table containing 30 records to support unified customer lifecycle and segmentation analysis.
