This data dictionary describes a paired collection of customer records from a subscription-based digital platform, encompassing account operational details alongside biographical and demographic profiles. The datasets may be joined via the S_N field, which establishes a one-to-one relationship between records, though a small number of entries on the biographical side lack matches and should be reviewed prior to integration; a secondary candidate linkage between system identifiers offers potential for fuzzy matching or record linkage comparison. The account table contributes subscription characteristics, user engagement metrics, and technical preferences while the biographical table supplies demographic attributes, contact information, and location data, enabling unified customer lifecycle and behavioral analysis. Each dataset contains 30 rows, with the account information spanning 15 columns organized around subscription and engagement categories and the biographical data comprising 12 columns focused on identity and address details.
