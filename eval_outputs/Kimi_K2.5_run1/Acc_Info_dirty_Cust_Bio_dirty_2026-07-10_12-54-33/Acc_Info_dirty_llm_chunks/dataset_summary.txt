This dataset comprises individual customer account records for a subscription-based digital platform, encompassing identity verification details, subscription tiers, and user engagement metrics. It supports customer relationship management and behavioral analytics such as subscription lifecycle tracking, churn analysis, and user preference profiling. The table structure includes 30 rows and 15 columns, with the ID field acting as a unique table-level identifier. Column groupings cover core identity attributes including names and account numbers, subscription characteristics and financial balances, temporal markers of account creation and recent activity, technical preferences regarding devices and operating systems, and customer satisfaction feedback scores.
