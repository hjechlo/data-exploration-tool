This data dictionary describes an integrated pair of customer datasets spanning account activity and biographical profiles for users of a subscription-based digital platform. The tables share a confirmed one-to-one relationship through a common serial number field, though a small number of records on the biographical side lack matching counterparts, and may be further compared using candidate identifier fields suitable for fuzzy matching or record linkage. Acc_Info_dirty supplies subscription tiers, financial balances, usage patterns, and technical preferences across fifteen columns, while Cust_Bio_dirty provides demographic attributes, contact details, and Singapore-based addressing information across twelve columns, with each dataset containing thirty rows of individual customer records.
