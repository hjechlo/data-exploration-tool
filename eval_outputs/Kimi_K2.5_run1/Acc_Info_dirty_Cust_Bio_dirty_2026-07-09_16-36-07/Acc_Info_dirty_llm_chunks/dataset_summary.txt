This dataset comprises account records for individual users of a subscription-based digital platform, capturing personal identifiers, subscription tiers, financial balances, and platform usage patterns across diverse operating systems and devices. It serves customer relationship management and user analytics use cases such as subscription revenue analysis, churn prediction, customer segmentation, and personalization based on language and device preferences. The dataset contains 30 rows and 15 columns, with both the ID and Full_Name fields serving as unique table-level identifiers, and is structured into logical groupings including demographic and account identifiers, temporal activity markers, subscription and financial attributes, technical preferences, and satisfaction metrics.
