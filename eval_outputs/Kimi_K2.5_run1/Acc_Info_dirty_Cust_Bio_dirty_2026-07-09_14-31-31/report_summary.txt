This report documents two complementary datasets capturing customer biographical details and account activity for a subscription-based digital platform. The tables share a high-confidence one-to-one linkage via the S_N identifier, allowing customer profiles to be joined with subscription parameters, financial balances, and engagement telemetry for integrated lifecycle analysis. Acc_Info_dirty comprises 30 rows and 15 columns spanning account management, subscription classifications, device specifications, and user feedback, while Cust_Bio_dirty contains 30 rows and 12 columns of personal identifiers, demographic attributes, and localized contact information.
