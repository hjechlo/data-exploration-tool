This dataset comprises individual customer account records for a subscription-based digital platform, encompassing personal identification details, subscription management parameters, and user engagement telemetry. It enables customer lifecycle analysis, subscription tier evaluation, and cross-platform usage pattern assessment for retention and satisfaction monitoring. The table structure includes 30 rows and 15 columns, with a unique table-level identifier field anchoring records that span personal identity and demographic attributes, account status and financial balances, subscription classifications and pricing, temporal activity indicators, device and operating system specifications, and customer feedback ratings.
