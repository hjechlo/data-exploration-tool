This collection brings together two customer-focused datasets for a subscription-based digital service: one centered on account, subscription, activity, device, and feedback records, and the other on customer biographical, demographic, contact, and location details. The tables are related through a shared S_N identifier that appears to support a largely one-to-one link, allowing account-level behavior and subscription context to be combined with customer profile information; in addition, account ID and identity-number fields may support further comparison or record linkage, but these should be treated as exploratory matching paths rather than confirmed joins. At a glance, Acc_Info_dirty contains 30 rows and 15 columns, while Cust_Bio_dirty contains 30 rows and 12 columns. Together, they provide a compact view of who the customers are, how to contact or segment them, and how their accounts are configured and used.
