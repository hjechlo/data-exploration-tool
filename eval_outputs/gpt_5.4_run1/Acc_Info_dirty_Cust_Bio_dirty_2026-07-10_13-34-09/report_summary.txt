This report covers two related datasets in a customer account management context for a subscription-based digital service, combining account-level operational records with customer biographical and contact information. The tables are linked through a shared S_N identifier that appears to support a largely one-to-one join, with the account table contributing enrollment, activity, subscription, balance, device, language, status, and feedback information, and the customer table contributing identity, demographic, contact, and address details; together they enable a more complete view of account holders and their service usage. A separate candidate linkage path between Acc_Info_dirty and Cust_Bio_dirty using ID and ID_No may support additional comparison or record linkage, but it should be treated as exploratory rather than a confirmed join. Structurally, Acc_Info_dirty contains 30 rows and 15 columns, while Cust_Bio_dirty contains 30 rows and 12 columns, with the collection organized around identifiers, customer profile information, account and subscription details, activity and financial measures, and location or contact attributes.
