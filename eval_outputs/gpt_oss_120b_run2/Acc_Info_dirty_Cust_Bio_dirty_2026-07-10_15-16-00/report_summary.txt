The collection comprises two tables that together capture a comprehensive view of individual customers for a subscription‑based service, spanning detailed account activity and core biographic profile information. They are linked by a confirmed one‑to‑one identifier (S_N) that appears in both tables, enabling a unified record per customer, while a secondary identifier pair (ID / ID_No) offers an exploratory linkage that may require fuzzy matching or additional validation. The account‑information table contains 30 rows and 15 columns grouped into demographics, subscription and financial details, activity timestamps, technology preferences and feedback metrics. The biographic table also holds 30 rows and 12 columns organized around unique identifiers, demographic attributes and contact information, together supporting reporting, segmentation and predictive analytics across the customer lifecycle.
