The Acc_Info_dirty table records individual customer accounts for a subscription‑based service, capturing personal identifiers, subscription choices, device usage and activity timestamps, placing it squarely in the customer‑relationship‑management domain. It is intended to support analyses of subscriber demographics, revenue contribution, engagement patterns and satisfaction, and can feed reporting dashboards, segmentation studies or predictive models for churn and upsell. The dataset is a flat table of 30 rows and 15 columns, with a unique identifier field (ID) that distinguishes each record. Columns are organized into logical groups such as demographic attributes, subscription and financial details, temporal activity markers, technology preferences and feedback scores.
