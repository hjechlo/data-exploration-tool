The collection comprises two “dirty” tables that together capture the customer‑relationship domain for a subscription‑based financial service, merging detailed account activity and subscription information with core demographic and contact data. They are linked by a confirmed one‑to‑one key on the shared S_N field, enabling a unified view of each customer’s account status, usage patterns, and personal profile, while a secondary candidate linkage using the numeric identifiers (ID versus ID_No) may be explored for fuzzy matching or record‑linkage purposes. Acc_Info_dirty holds 30 rows and 15 columns organized into demographic attributes, account and subscription details, temporal fields, technology usage, and feedback metrics; Cust_Bio_dirty also contains 30 rows with 12 columns grouped around personal identifiers, demographic attributes, and contact details. Together, the integrated view supports downstream analytics such as usage reporting, churn prediction, and targeted communications, making it directly relevant to data practitioners focused on customer insight and operational reporting.
