The table captures a snapshot of individual customers, recording personal identifiers together with demographic and contact information, placing it squarely in the customer‑relationship domain of a financial services organization. It is intended to support activities such as customer profiling, segmentation, compliance checks, and targeted communications within banking or similar service systems. The dataset comprises 30 rows and 12 columns, anchored by a unique numeric identifier that distinguishes each record, and the fields can be broadly grouped into personal identifiers, demographic attributes (e.g., race, gender, age) and contact details (address, phone, email, country).
