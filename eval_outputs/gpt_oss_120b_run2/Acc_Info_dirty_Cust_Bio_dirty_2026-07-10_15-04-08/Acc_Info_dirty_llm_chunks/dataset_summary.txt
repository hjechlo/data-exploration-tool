The Acc_Info_dirty table captures information about individual customer accounts for a subscription‑based service, recording personal identifiers, subscription tier, device usage and feedback. It is intended to support customer‑relationship management, billing, usage analytics and segmentation activities that rely on a view of each account’s status, activity dates and satisfaction scores. The dataset consists of 30 rows and 15 columns, with the string field ID acting as a unique table‑level identifier. The columns can be grouped into demographic attributes (e.g., name, preferred language), account and subscription details (account number, balance, subscription type and fee, status), temporal fields (join date, last login), technology usage (device, operating system) and feedback metrics (two score fields).
