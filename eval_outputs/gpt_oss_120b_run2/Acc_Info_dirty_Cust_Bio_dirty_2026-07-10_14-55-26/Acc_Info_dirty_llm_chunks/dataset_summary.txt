The Acc_Info_dirty table records individual customer accounts for a subscription‑based service, capturing personal identifiers, subscription tier and fee, account balance, activity timestamps, device and operating‑system details, and user‑provided feedback scores. It is intended to support customer‑relationship management, billing operations, usage analytics, and satisfaction monitoring within a consumer‑services domain. The dataset comprises 30 rows and 15 columns, with the string field ID acting as a unique table‑level identifier; the remaining fields cluster around personal demographics, account and subscription attributes, login and join dates, technology usage, and feedback metrics.
