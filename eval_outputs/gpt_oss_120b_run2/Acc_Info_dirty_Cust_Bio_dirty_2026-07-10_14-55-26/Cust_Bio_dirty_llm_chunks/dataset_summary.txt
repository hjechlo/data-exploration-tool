The Cust_Bio_dirty table captures basic biographical information for individual customers, recording identifiers such as account and national‑ID numbers together with personal attributes like name, race, gender, and age, placing it squarely in the customer‑relationship domain of a financial or service‑provider organization. It is intended to support downstream activities such as client onboarding, demographic profiling, targeted communications, and compliance checks that require a consolidated view of each customer’s identity and contact details. The dataset comprises 30 rows and 12 columns, featuring a unique row‑level identifier (S_N) and three logical groupings: personal identifiers, demographic characteristics, and contact/location fields such as phone, email, street name, postal code and country.
