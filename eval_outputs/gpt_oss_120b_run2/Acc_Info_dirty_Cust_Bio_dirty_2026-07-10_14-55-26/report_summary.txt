The collection comprises two tables that together capture a comprehensive view of customers for a subscription‑based service, merging account‑level activity and billing details with biographical and demographic information. The tables can be linked through a confirmed one‑to‑one relationship on the shared S_N identifier, allowing each account record to be matched to its corresponding personal profile, while an additional exploratory linkage using the ID fields may support fuzzy matching or supplemental checks. The account table contains 30 rows and 15 columns organized around personal identifiers, subscription attributes, usage timestamps, technology details, and satisfaction metrics, whereas the biographical table also holds 30 rows with 12 columns grouped into identifiers, demographic characteristics, and contact/location fields. Together, the combined view enables downstream analyses such as customer‑lifecycle reporting, targeted communications, compliance verification, and revenue analytics across the full customer base.
