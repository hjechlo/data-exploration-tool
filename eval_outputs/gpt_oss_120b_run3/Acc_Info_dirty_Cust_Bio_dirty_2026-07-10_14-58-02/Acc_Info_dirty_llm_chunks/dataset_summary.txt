The Acc_Info_dirty table records individual customer accounts for a subscription‑based service, capturing personal identifiers, subscription tier and fees, account balances, device and operating‑system usage, and periodic feedback scores. It is intended to support operational management of accounts, billing and revenue reporting, as well as analytical work such as usage‑pattern, churn and segmentation modeling. The dataset consists of 30 rows and 15 columns; it contains a unique table‑level identifier (the ID field) together with a uniquely identifying full‑name column, and the remaining fields fall into logical groups covering demographic attributes, subscription and financial details, activity timestamps (join date and last login), and technology‑usage plus feedback metrics.
