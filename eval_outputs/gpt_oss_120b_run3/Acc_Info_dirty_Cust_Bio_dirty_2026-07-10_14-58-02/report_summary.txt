The collection comprises two complementary tables that together describe individual customers of a subscription‑based financial service, merging operational account details with biographical and contact information. The tables are linked by a confirmed one‑to‑one identifier (the S_N field), enabling a unified view of each customer, while an additional identifier pair (ID versus ID_No) is identified as a candidate linkage that may support fuzzy or record‑linkage techniques after further validation. Acc_Info_dirty holds 30 rows and 15 columns, organized into logical groups covering demographics, subscription tier and fees, activity timestamps, technology usage, and feedback metrics. Cust_Bio_dirty also contains 30 rows and 12 columns, grouped around demographics, account identifiers, and contact/location details.
