The collection comprises two tables that together capture the full profile of individual customers for a subscription‑based service, spanning the customer‑relationship‑management and financial‑services domains. The tables are linked by a shared one‑to‑one identifier (S_N), which allows a direct join of each account record to its corresponding biographical record; a second set of identifier fields provides a candidate pathway for fuzzy matching if required. Acc_Info_dirty contains 30 rows and 15 columns organized around demographic attributes, account details, activity timestamps and satisfaction feedback, while Cust_Bio_dirty holds 30 rows and 12 columns grouped into demographics, contact information, location data and account identifiers. Together they enable analyses such as segmentation, revenue forecasting, churn risk assessment, and compliance checks across the combined customer view.
