The Acc_Info_dirty table captures information about individual customer accounts for a subscription‑based service, describing personal identifiers, subscription choices, usage activity and satisfaction feedback, placing it in the customer relationship‑management and subscription‑service domain. It is intended to support analyses of customer demographics, subscription revenue, engagement patterns, churn risk and feedback‑driven improvement, making it useful for reporting, segmentation and predictive modelling. The dataset comprises 30 rows and 15 columns, with a unique table‑level identifier field (ID) that distinguishes each record. The columns can be broadly grouped into demographic attributes (e.g., name, preferred language, device, operating system), account details (account number, balance, subscription type and fee, status), temporal activity fields (join date, last login) and feedback scores.
