The Acc_Info_dirty table records individual customer accounts for a subscription‑based service, capturing personal identifiers, subscription tier, usage activity and feedback, and therefore belongs to the customer‑relationship‑management domain. It is intended to support account administration, billing, engagement analysis and segmentation tasks such as revenue reporting or churn modelling. The dataset comprises 30 rows and 15 columns, with the string field ID acting as a unique table‑level identifier; the columns are logically grouped into personal demographics, account and financial attributes, activity timestamps, and feedback and device‑usage metrics.
