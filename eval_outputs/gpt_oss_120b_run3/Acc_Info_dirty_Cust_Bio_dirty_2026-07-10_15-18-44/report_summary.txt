The collection comprises two tables that together capture the core customer‑relationship‑management data for a subscription‑based service, one holding account‑level details and the other holding biographical and contact information. The tables are linked by a confirmed one‑to‑one identifier (the shared S_N field), enabling a direct merge of account activity with customer demographics, while an additional candidate linkage using alternative identifier fields can be explored with fuzzy‑matching techniques. At a glance, each table contains 30 records; the account table provides fifteen attributes grouped into demographics, financial and usage metrics, and the biographical table supplies twelve attributes organized around personal demographics, an account reference, and contact/location details.
