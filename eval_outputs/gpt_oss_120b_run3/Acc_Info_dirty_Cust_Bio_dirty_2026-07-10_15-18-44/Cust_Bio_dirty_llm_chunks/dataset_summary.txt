The Cust_Bio_dirty table records biographical and contact details for individual customers, placing it in the customer‑relationship domain. It is intended to support profiling, marketing, compliance, or account‑management activities where a view of each customer’s personal attributes and contact information is required. The dataset comprises 30 rows and 12 columns; the integer field S_N uniquely identifies each record, while the remaining columns are grouped into demographic attributes (Name, ID_No, Race, Gender, Age), an account reference (Account_No), and contact/location fields (Contact_No, Email, Street_Name, Postal_Code, Country).
