This data dictionary describes an integrated customer record system from a subscription-based digital service, pairing account operational data with biographical and contact information to support comprehensive relationship management and demographic analysis. The two datasets are linked by a shared serial number identifier that establishes a one-to-one relationship, enabling the combination of subscription behavior, financial attributes, and device usage with residential and demographic profiles, while additional candidate identifier fields offer potential for fuzzy matching or record linkage workflows. Acc_Info_dirty comprises 30 rows and 15 columns spanning subscription characteristics, temporal activity markers, technical specifications, and satisfaction metrics, while Cust_Bio_dirty contains 30 rows and 12 columns organized around demographic attributes, contact details, and address information.
