This dataset comprises individual user account records for a subscription-based digital service, capturing customer identity information, subscription details, and platform usage characteristics across 30 records and 15 attributes. It supports customer relationship management and user behavior analysis, enabling segmentation by subscription tier, device ecosystem, and engagement history to inform retention strategies and service optimization efforts. The data is structured with a unique table-level identifier in the ID field and organized into thematic clusters including personal identifiers and demographics, subscription and financial attributes, temporal activity markers, technical device specifications, and customer satisfaction metrics.
