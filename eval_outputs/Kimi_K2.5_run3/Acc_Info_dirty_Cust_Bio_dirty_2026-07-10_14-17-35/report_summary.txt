This data dictionary describes a paired collection of customer records spanning account activity and demographic profiles for a Singapore-based subscription digital service. The two tables share a standardized identifier that functions as a confirmed one-to-one join key, enabling precise linkage of subscription and platform usage details to biographical and contact information, though analysts should note that a small subset of demographic records lack corresponding account entries; additional candidate fields support fuzzy matching or record linkage validation between the datasets. The account information table contains 30 rows and 15 columns organized around subscription management, financial balances, technical platform specifications, and customer satisfaction metrics, while the demographic table comprises 30 rows and 12 columns covering personal identity attributes, contact details, and residential addressing.
