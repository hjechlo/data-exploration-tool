This dataset comprises individual customer account records for a subscription-based digital service, capturing personal identifiers, subscription details, account balances, platform usage characteristics, and customer satisfaction metrics. It supports customer relationship management and business intelligence applications, including analysis of subscription revenue patterns, user engagement trends, platform preferences across devices and operating systems, and customer retention strategies. The dataset contains 30 rows and 15 columns, with both the standardized identification field and the full name field serving as unique table-level identifiers. The structure encompasses demographic and identity attributes, subscription and financial details, technical platform specifications, temporal activity markers, and feedback ratings.
