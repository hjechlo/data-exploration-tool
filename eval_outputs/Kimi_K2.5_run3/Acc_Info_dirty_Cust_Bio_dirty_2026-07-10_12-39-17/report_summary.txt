This collection comprises customer data for a Singapore-based digital subscription service, spanning both account activity records and comprehensive biographical profiles. The two datasets are designed to be joined one-to-one via a shared national identifier field, linking subscription details and platform usage history with complete contact information and demographic attributes, though users should note that a small subset of biographical records currently lack corresponding account entries. Additionally, the datasets may be compared or linked using candidate identifier and fuzzy matching attributes to support alternative record linkage strategies. Acc_Info_dirty contains 30 rows and 15 columns capturing subscription tiers, temporal activity markers, and technical preferences, while Cust_Bio_dirty contains 30 rows and 12 columns housing demographic characteristics and full residential contact details.
