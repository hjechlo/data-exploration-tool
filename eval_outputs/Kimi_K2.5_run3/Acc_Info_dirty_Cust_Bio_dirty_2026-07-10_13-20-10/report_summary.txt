This data dictionary describes a paired collection of customer records for a subscription-based digital service, combining account management data with biographical and contact information. The two tables share a common serial number identifier that supports a one-to-one linkage, enabling integration of subscription details, financial status, and behavioral metrics with demographic attributes, contact information, and Singapore-based location data; additional candidate identifier fields suggest potential for fuzzy matching or record linkage workflows. Both datasets contain 30 rows, with the account information spanning 15 columns organized around membership characteristics, account status, and user engagement patterns, while the biographical data comprises 12 columns covering personal identification, demographics, communication details, and residential address information.
