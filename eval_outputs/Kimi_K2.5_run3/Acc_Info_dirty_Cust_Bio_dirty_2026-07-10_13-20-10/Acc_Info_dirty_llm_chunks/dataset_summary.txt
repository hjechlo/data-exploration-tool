This dataset captures individual customer account records for a subscription-based digital service, spanning identity verification, membership details, and user engagement metrics. It serves customer relationship management and behavioral analytics use cases such as subscription tier analysis, churn prediction, and user segmentation based on platform usage patterns and satisfaction ratings. The table contains 30 rows and 15 columns, with Full_Name and ID serving as unique table-level identifiers. The data is organized into four main groupings: demographic and identity attributes including government identification numbers and language preferences; subscription characteristics covering tier levels and associated fees; account status indicators and financial balances paired with enrollment dates; and behavioral fields capturing device preferences, operating systems, login activity, and feedback scores.
