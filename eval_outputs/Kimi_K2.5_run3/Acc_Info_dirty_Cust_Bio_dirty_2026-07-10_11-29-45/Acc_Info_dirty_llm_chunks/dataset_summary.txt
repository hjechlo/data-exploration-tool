This dataset describes individual customer accounts for a subscription-based digital service, capturing personal identifiers, subscription details, and platform usage characteristics within a Southeast Asian market context evidenced by Singapore-specific identification numbers and multilingual language preferences. The data supports customer lifecycle management and subscription analytics, enabling retention analysis, tier distribution studies, and platform optimization based on device and operating system usage patterns. Spanning 30 rows and 15 columns, the dataset features Full_Name and ID as unique table-level identifiers, organized into identity attributes, subscription management fields, temporal activity tracking, and technical preference indicators including device types and satisfaction metrics.
