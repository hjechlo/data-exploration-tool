This collection comprises customer data for a Singapore-based subscription digital service, encompassing both operational account information and biographical registry records to support comprehensive customer analytics and identity management. The datasets connect through a confirmed one-to-one relationship on the S_N identifier, linking subscription activity to demographic and contact profiles, though a small proportion of biographical records may lack corresponding account matches. Additional candidate linkage paths suggest potential for fuzzy matching or record linkage using alternative identifier fields, enabling flexible alignment strategies beyond the primary key. Acc_Info_dirty contributes subscription details and platform usage characteristics while Cust_Bio_dirty supplies demographic attributes and location data, together enabling unified lifecycle analysis and customer segmentation. Each dataset contains 30 records, with Acc_Info_dirty structured across 15 columns covering subscription management and technical preferences, and Cust_Bio_dirty spanning 12 columns organized around identity verification, demographics, and contact information.
