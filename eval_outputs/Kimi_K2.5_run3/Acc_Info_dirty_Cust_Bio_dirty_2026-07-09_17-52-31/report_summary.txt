This data dictionary describes a paired collection of customer records for a subscription-based digital platform, combining account activity and subscription details with biographical and contact information for individuals located in Singapore. The two tables share the S_N identifier, which establishes a one-to-one relationship between account records and customer biographical profiles, enabling integrated analysis of subscription behavior alongside demographic attributes and location data, though a small number of biographical entries lack corresponding account matches. A candidate linkage path between additional identifier fields may support fuzzy matching or record linkage exploration beyond the primary join. Together the datasets comprise 30 rows each, with the account information table containing 15 columns spanning subscription financials, activity timelines, and technical specifications, while the biographical table offers 12 columns organized around demographics, contact details, and residential address information.
