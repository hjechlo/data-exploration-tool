This dataset captures individual customer account information for a subscription-based digital platform, encompassing demographic details, subscription configurations, and user activity patterns across 30 records. It serves customer relationship management and subscription analytics use cases, supporting analysis of revenue streams, user engagement lifecycles, and account status transitions. The dataset contains 15 columns including unique table-level identifiers in the ID and Full_Name fields, with attributes organized into demographic and language preferences, subscription financials and account balances, temporal activity markers such as join dates and last logins, technical specifications covering devices and operating systems, and customer feedback metrics.
