This data dictionary describes a pair of customer datasets from a subscription-based service platform, combining account activity records with biographical and contact information to support customer relationship management and subscription analytics. The tables share a common serial number field that functions as a near-complete one-to-one join key, allowing subscription tiers and engagement metrics to be linked with demographic details and contact data, while a secondary candidate identifier pair offers potential for fuzzy matching or consistency validation. Acc_Info_dirty contains 30 rows and 15 columns organized around subscription characteristics, financial balances, and behavioral metrics, while Cust_Bio_dirty contains 30 rows and 12 columns covering personal identifiers, demographic attributes, and Singapore location data, together enabling integrated analysis of user retention, revenue patterns, and customer segmentation.
