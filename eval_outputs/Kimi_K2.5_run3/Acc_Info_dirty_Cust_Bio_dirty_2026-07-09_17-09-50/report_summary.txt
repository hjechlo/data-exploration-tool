This data dictionary describes a paired collection of customer records for a Singapore-based subscription digital service, spanning account management and biographical identity data. The tables relate through the S_N identifier, which functions as a one-to-one join key enabling integration of subscription activity, device usage, and financial details with verified customer demographics and residential addresses, though the biographical table contains a small number of records without corresponding account entries. A candidate linkage path between the tables' numeric identifier fields may support additional fuzzy matching or record linkage workflows. The account information table provides 30 rows and 15 columns covering subscription tiers, temporal milestones, and technical characteristics, while the biographical table offers 30 rows and 12 columns of personal identifiers, demographic attributes, and Singapore location data, together enabling comprehensive customer analytics, identity verification, and retention analysis across the user base.
