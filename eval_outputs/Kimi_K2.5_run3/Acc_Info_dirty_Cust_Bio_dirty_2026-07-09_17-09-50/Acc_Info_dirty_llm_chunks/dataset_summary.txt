This dataset contains customer account information for a subscription-based digital service, describing individual user accounts with attributes suggesting a Southeast Asian, likely Singaporean, user base indicated by National Registration Identity Card numbers and multicultural naming patterns. The data serves customer relationship management and subscription analytics purposes, supporting analyses of user retention, subscription tier performance, engagement patterns, and device preferences across the customer base. Comprising 30 rows and 15 columns, the dataset features a unique table-level identifier in the ID field, with column groupings encompassing demographic and identity attributes, temporal account milestones such as join and login dates, subscription and financial details including tier and balance, and technical characteristics covering device type, operating system, and user feedback metrics.
