This dataset comprises biographical records for individual customers located in Singapore, encompassing personal identifiers, demographic attributes, and residential contact information. It is designed to support customer identity verification, demographic analysis, and communication workflows within service-oriented business systems. The table contains 30 rows and 12 columns, structured with a numeric identifier field serving as the unique table-level identifier, alongside column groupings covering personal and account identifiers, demographic characteristics, contact details, and Singapore street addresses with postal codes.
