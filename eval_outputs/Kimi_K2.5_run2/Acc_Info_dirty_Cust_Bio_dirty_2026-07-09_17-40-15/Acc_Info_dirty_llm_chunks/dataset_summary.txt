This dataset captures individual customer account records for a subscription-based digital service, encompassing personal identifiers, demographic attributes such as language preference, and account lifecycle details spanning from 2020 to 2027. The data supports customer relationship management, subscription analytics, and user behavior analysis for tracking membership tiers, financial balances, and platform engagement across desktop and mobile environments. Structured with 30 rows and 15 columns, the dataset contains unique table-level identifiers in the ID and Full_Name fields, alongside column groupings covering personal demographics, account timestamps and status indicators, subscription types and fees, financial balances, device and operating system specifications, and user feedback scores.
