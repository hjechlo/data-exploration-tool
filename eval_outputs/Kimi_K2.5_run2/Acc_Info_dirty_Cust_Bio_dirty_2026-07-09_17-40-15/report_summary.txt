This data dictionary describes a paired collection of customer records spanning account management and biographical verification for a subscription-based digital service operating within a Singapore-centric domain. The two tables connect through a shared serial number identifier that establishes a confirmed one-to-one relationship between account lifecycle data and personal contact records, enabling unified analysis of subscription behavior alongside verified demographics and location details, though users should note that a small subset of biographical records currently lack matching account entries. Additional candidate identifier fields present opportunities for exploratory record linkage or fuzzy matching between the datasets. Acc_Info_dirty comprises 30 rows and 15 columns capturing personal demographics, account timestamps and status indicators, subscription types and fees, financial balances, device specifications, and user feedback scores, while Cust_Bio_dirty contains 30 rows and 12 columns organized into identification fields, demographic characteristics, contact information, and residential address components.
