This data dictionary describes a pair of customer datasets from a subscription-based digital service, combining account activity records with biographical profiles to support integrated customer analytics. The two tables share a common serial number identifier that enables a one-to-one linkage between subscription details and demographic information, while candidate identifier fields provide exploratory paths for fuzzy matching or record linkage. The account information table contains 30 rows and 15 columns covering subscription metrics, device specifications, and engagement data, while the biographical dataset comprises 30 rows and 12 columns focused on demographic characteristics and contact details.
