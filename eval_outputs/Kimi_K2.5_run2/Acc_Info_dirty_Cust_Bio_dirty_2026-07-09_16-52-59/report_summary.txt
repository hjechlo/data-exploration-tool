This data dictionary describes an integrated pair of customer datasets spanning digital subscription accounts and biographical demographic information for individuals predominantly based in Singapore. The tables may be joined one-to-one through a shared national identifier field, though analysts should verify a small number of unmatched biographical records before relying on this key; together they enable linking subscription behavior and platform engagement to customer demographics and contact details for enriched customer relationship management. The account table comprises thirty rows and fifteen columns covering subscription configuration, activity history, technical preferences, and satisfaction metrics, while the biographical table contains thirty rows and twelve columns organized around national identifiers, personal characteristics, and contact information. A secondary candidate linkage path between system identifiers offers potential for fuzzy matching or record linkage exploration, though this relationship remains tentative compared to the primary join key.
