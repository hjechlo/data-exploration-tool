This dataset comprises individual account records for a subscription-based digital service, capturing registered users alongside their membership details, platform preferences, and activity history. It serves customer relationship management and subscription analytics use cases, supporting analyses of user engagement, subscription tier distribution, and account lifecycle patterns. The dataset contains 30 rows and 15 columns, with the ID field serving as a unique table-level identifier, and is structured around five thematic domains: personal identification attributes, subscription configuration and billing details, temporal activity markers, technical platform preferences, and customer satisfaction metrics.
