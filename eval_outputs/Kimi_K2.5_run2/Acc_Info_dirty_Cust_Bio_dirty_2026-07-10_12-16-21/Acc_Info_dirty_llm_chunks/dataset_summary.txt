This dataset describes individual customer accounts for a subscription-based digital platform, capturing personal identifiers, demographic attributes, and service usage patterns across multiple membership tiers including Basic, Premium, and Free subscriptions. It serves customer relationship management and behavioral analytics use cases such as churn prediction, subscription revenue analysis, and platform engagement monitoring based on login activity and device preferences. The dataset contains 30 rows and 15 columns, with unique table-level identifiers present in the ID and Full_Name fields, organized into thematic groupings covering identity information, subscription and financial details, temporal activity markers, technical specifications, and customer feedback metrics.
