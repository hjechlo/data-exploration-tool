This data dictionary describes a paired collection of customer records for a subscription-based digital platform, spanning account activity and biographical profile information. The datasets align on a shared serial number field that supports a one-to-one linkage, though a small number of records in the biographical table lack matches and should be reviewed prior to integration; a secondary candidate linkage path between identifier fields offers potential for fuzzy matching or record linkage operations. Acc_Info_dirty contributes subscription details, usage patterns, and service metrics, while Cust_Bio_dirty supplies demographic attributes, contact information, and Singapore-based address data, together enabling integrated customer relationship management and behavioral analytics. Each dataset contains 30 records, with Acc_Info_dirty comprising 15 columns organized around identity, subscription and financial details, activity markers, and technical specifications, and Cust_Bio_dirty containing 12 columns covering system identifiers, personal demographics, contact details, and physical location.
