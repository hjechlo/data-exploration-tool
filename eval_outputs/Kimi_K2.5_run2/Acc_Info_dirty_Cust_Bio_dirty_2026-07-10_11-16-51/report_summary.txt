This data dictionary describes a paired collection of customer datasets for a digital subscription service, comprising account operational records and biographical registry information focused on Singapore-based individuals. The two tables share the S_N identifier as a confirmed one-to-one join key, enabling the linkage of subscription and platform usage details with demographic profiles and residential addresses to support unified customer lifecycle analysis and identity verification workflows. Additionally, candidate linkage paths exist via supplementary identifier fields that may facilitate fuzzy matching or probabilistic record linkage techniques for comparing records across the datasets. Acc_Info_dirty contains 30 rows and 15 columns spanning identity attributes, temporal markers, subscription and financial details, technical specifications, and customer feedback metrics, while Cust_Bio_dirty contains 30 rows and 12 columns organized around personal identification credentials, demographic characteristics, contact channels, and residential address components.
