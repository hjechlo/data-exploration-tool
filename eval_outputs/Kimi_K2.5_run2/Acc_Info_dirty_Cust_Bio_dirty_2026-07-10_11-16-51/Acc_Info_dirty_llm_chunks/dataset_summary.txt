This dataset describes individual customer accounts for a digital subscription service, encompassing personal identifiers, membership details, and platform usage characteristics. It supports customer lifecycle management, subscription revenue analysis, and user engagement tracking across varying service tiers and technical environments. The dataset structure comprises 30 rows and 15 columns, where ID and Full_Name function as unique table-level identifiers, and fields are organized into identity and demographic attributes, temporal account markers, subscription and financial details, technical specifications, and customer feedback metrics.
