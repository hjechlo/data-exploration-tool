This collection comprises customer data for a subscription-based digital service, pairing account activity and subscription metrics with biographical and contact information. The two tables share the S_N field as a confirmed one-to-one join key linking account records to customer profiles, though a small proportion of biographical records lack matching counterparts likely due to duplicates or unmatched entries; additionally, the datasets may be compared or linked using candidate identifier attributes for fuzzy matching or record linkage purposes. Acc_Info_dirty contributes subscription tiers, financial details, platform engagement metrics, and satisfaction scores while Cust_Bio_dirty supplies demographic attributes, contact details, and residential addresses, enabling integrated analysis of customer identity alongside service usage patterns. Both tables contain 30 rows, with the account information spanning 15 columns across administrative, subscription, technical preference, and satisfaction categories, and the biographical data organized into 12 columns covering identifiers, demographics, contact information, and physical location components.
