This dataset describes individual customer accounts for a subscription-based digital service, encompassing personal identifiers, subscription details, and platform engagement metrics. It supports customer relationship management, subscription analytics, and user behavior analysis for initiatives such as churn prediction, customer lifetime value modeling, and service personalization. The table contains 30 rows and 15 columns, with ID functioning as a unique table-level identifier. The columns cluster into five thematic areas: personal identification attributes, account administrative timestamps and status indicators, subscription tier and financial details, technical device and language preferences, and customer satisfaction metrics.
