This data dictionary describes a paired collection of Singapore-based customer records for a digital service platform, spanning account operational data and biographical contact details. The tables may be joined one-to-one via a shared identifier that aligns nearly all records between the account and customer datasets, though a small number of biographical entries lack corresponding account matches and should be reviewed before integration; additionally, candidate identifier fields may support fuzzy matching or record linkage workflows for alternative alignment strategies. Acc_Info_dirty contributes subscription details, financial balances, technical engagement metrics, and account status indicators, while Cust_Bio_dirty supplies demographic attributes, contact coordinates, and location data, enabling unified customer analytics that unite behavioral patterns with identity profiles. The account table contains 30 rows and 15 columns organized around personal identification, subscription management, financial status, and technical usage groupings, while the biographical table contains 30 rows and 12 columns covering identity verification, demographics, contact information, and address fields.
