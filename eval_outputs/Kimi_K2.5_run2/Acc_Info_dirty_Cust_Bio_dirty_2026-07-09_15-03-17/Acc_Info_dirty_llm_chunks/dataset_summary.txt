This dataset captures individual customer account records for a digital service platform, encompassing personal identifiers, subscription tiers, and engagement metrics within the customer relationship management domain. It serves customer analytics use cases such as subscription management, churn prediction, and user segmentation based on engagement patterns, device preferences, and financial value. The dataset comprises 30 rows and 15 columns organized around a unique table-level identifier, with attribute groupings covering demographic and identification details, subscription and billing information, temporal activity indicators, technical device specifications, and customer satisfaction metrics.
