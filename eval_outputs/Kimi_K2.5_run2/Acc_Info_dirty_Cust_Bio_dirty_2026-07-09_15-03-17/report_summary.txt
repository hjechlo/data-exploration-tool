This data dictionary describes a paired collection of customer records from a digital service platform, combining account activity data with biographical and contact information likely pertaining to a Singapore-based customer base. The two datasets are designed to be joined one-to-one on the shared S_N identifier, with the account information table contributing subscription tiers, engagement metrics, and technical specifications while the biographical table provides demographic attributes, contact details, and physical addresses; together they enable comprehensive customer analytics and identity verification workflows. A candidate linkage path also exists between additional identifier fields that may support fuzzy matching or record linkage exploration. Both datasets contain 30 rows, with the account table comprising 15 columns organized around activity and billing categories and the biographical table containing 12 columns covering personal and address information, though users should note that a small subset of records in the biographical dataset lack matching keys in the account data.
