The collection comprises two complementary tables that together capture the full profile of customers for a subscription‑based service, merging operational account data with biographic and contact information. The tables are linked by a confirmed one‑to‑one relationship on the shared S_N identifier, enabling a reliable join of each account record to its corresponding biographic record, while an additional candidate linkage using the ID/ID_No fields may be explored with fuzzy‑matching techniques. Acc_Info_dirty contains 30 rows and 15 columns organized into groups such as personal attributes, subscription details, activity timestamps, device/OS information and feedback scores; Cust_Bio_dirty also holds 30 rows with 12 columns grouped into account‑related identifiers, demographic attributes, and contact/location details. Together they support billing, account management, CRM, KYC, and analytical use cases ranging from subscription tier performance to targeted marketing segmentation.
