The Acc_Info_dirty table records individual customer accounts for a subscription‑based service, capturing personal identifiers, subscription choices, usage timestamps and feedback. It is intended to support operational functions such as billing and account management and to enable analytical work on subscription tier performance, user engagement and satisfaction. The data are presented as a single flat table with 30 rows and 15 columns; each record is uniquely identified by the ID field and the remaining columns fall into groups such as personal attributes, subscription details, temporal activity markers, device/OS information and feedback scores.
