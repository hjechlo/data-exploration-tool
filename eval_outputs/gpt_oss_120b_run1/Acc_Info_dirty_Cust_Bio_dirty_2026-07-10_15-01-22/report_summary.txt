The collection consists of two tables that together represent the full customer universe for a subscription‑based service, merging account‑level activity with core biographic profiles. The tables share a confirmed one‑to‑one relationship on the S_N field, enabling each account record to be matched directly to its corresponding biographic record; additional identifiers such as ID and ID_No are noted as candidate linkage paths that could support fuzzy matching or record‑linkage in exploratory work. This combined view supports analyses of customer demographics, subscription revenue, engagement patterns, and satisfaction, facilitating segmentation, churn prediction, and operational reporting. At a glance, each table holds 30 rows; the account table contains 15 columns grouped into personal demographics, account metadata, subscription attributes, interaction timestamps, device information and feedback scores, while the biographic table includes 12 columns organized around identifier fields, demographic attributes and contact details.
