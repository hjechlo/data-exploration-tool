The Acc_Info_dirty table records individual customer accounts for a subscription‑based service, capturing personal identifiers, subscription details, usage activity and feedback. It is intended to support analyses of customer demographics, subscription revenue, engagement patterns, and satisfaction, enabling segmentation, churn prediction and service‑performance reporting. The dataset comprises 30 rows and 15 columns, with each record uniquely identified by the ID field; the columns fall into logical groups such as personal demographics (full name, preferred language), account metadata (account number, join date, status), subscription attributes (type, fee, balance), interaction timestamps (last login), device and operating‑system information, and numeric feedback scores.
