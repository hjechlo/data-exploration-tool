The collection comprises two complementary datasets that together describe customers of a subscription‑based service and a financial institution, providing both account‑level activity details and biographical/contact information. The tables can be linked through a shared one‑to‑one identifier (S_N), enabling a unified view of each individual’s subscription behavior alongside their personal and address attributes, while a secondary candidate linkage using the generic ID fields may support additional matching or record‑linkage techniques after validation. The account‑information table contains 30 rows and 15 columns organized into logical groups such as demographic attributes, subscription tier and fees, usage timestamps, device/operating‑system preferences, and feedback measurements. The biographical table also holds 30 rows and 12 columns, grouped into personal identifiers, account reference, demographic traits, contact channels, and location details.
