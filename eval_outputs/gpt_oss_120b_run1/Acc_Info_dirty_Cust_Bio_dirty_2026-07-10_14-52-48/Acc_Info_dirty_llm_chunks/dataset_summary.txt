The Acc_Info_dirty table captures information about individual customer accounts for a subscription‑based service, recording personal identifiers, subscription tier and fees, activity timestamps, device and operating‑system usage, and feedback scores. It is intended to support customer‑centric analytics such as revenue reporting, usage and engagement analysis, and segmentation for personalized marketing or service improvement. The dataset comprises 30 rows and 15 columns, with the ID field serving as a unique table‑level identifier; the remaining fields fall into logical groups covering demographic attributes, account and subscription details, login and join dates, technology preferences, and feedback measurements.
