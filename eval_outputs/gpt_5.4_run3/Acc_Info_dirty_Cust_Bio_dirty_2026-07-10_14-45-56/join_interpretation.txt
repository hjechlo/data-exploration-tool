Exact resemblance measures how much the distinct values in two columns overlap, while shingle resemblance measures fuzzy character-level similarity and is mainly useful for text fields such as names or addresses; directional containment instead asks whether values in one column are contained within another, which is the key signal for confirming foreign key relationships. In these results, Acc_Info_dirty.S_N and Cust_Bio_dirty.S_N behave like a confirmed one-to-one shared key, with very strong overlap and near-complete coverage, although the small unmatched portion on Cust_Bio_dirty.S_N should be checked before relying on it operationally. By contrast, Acc_Info_dirty.ID and Cust_Bio_dirty.ID_No is only an exploratory candidate linkage path from MinHash and should be treated as a possible linkage or blocking field until validated, not as a confirmed primary/foreign key relationship. More generally, shared domains and candidate similarities should not be described as PK/FK joins unless directional containment or referential integrity confirms them, and low exact resemblance on a numeric key column does not by itself mean fuzzy matching is needed when referential integrity is strong.
