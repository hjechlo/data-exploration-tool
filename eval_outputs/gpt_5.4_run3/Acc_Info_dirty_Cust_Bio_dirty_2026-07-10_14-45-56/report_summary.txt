This report covers two related customer-level datasets from an account-based digital subscription service: one centered on account, subscription, activity, device, and feedback information, and the other on customer biographical, demographic, contact, and address details. The tables are linked by a shared S_N identifier that appears to support a largely one-to-one relationship, allowing account records to be combined with customer profile information for a fuller view of each customer; in addition, ID and ID_No appear to be a candidate linkage path for comparison or validation rather than a confirmed join. At a glance, Acc_Info_dirty contains 30 rows and 15 columns, while Cust_Bio_dirty contains 30 rows and 12 columns. Together, they support customer administration, lifecycle and engagement analysis, subscription reporting, and profile-based segmentation across operational and analytical use cases.
