Exact resemblance measures how much the distinct values in two columns overlap, while shingle resemblance measures fuzzy character-level similarity and is mainly useful for text fields such as names or addresses; directional containment instead asks whether values in one column are contained within the other, which is the key signal for confirming foreign keys.
