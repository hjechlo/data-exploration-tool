This report covers two related customer datasets for a subscription-based digital service: one focused on account administration and service usage, and the other on customer biographical and contact details. The tables are designed to work together through a shared S_N identifier that appears to support a largely one-to-one link, with the account table contributing subscription, balance, activity, device, preference, and feedback information and the customer table contributing identity, demographic, and address/contact context; combined, they enable a more complete customer-level view for administration, reporting, and analysis. In addition, account ID and personal ID number fields appear to be candidate linkage paths for comparison or record linkage, but they should not be treated as a confirmed join. At a glance, Acc_Info_dirty contains 30 rows and 15 columns, Cust_Bio_dirty contains 30 rows and 12 columns, and together they span customer identity and reference information, account and subscription details, financial and status measures, device and preference attributes, activity timing, and contact and location information.
